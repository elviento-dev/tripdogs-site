Reflections on Marcus Aurelius and Philosophy

Published: 2026-10-05

My Perspective [#]

Marcus Aurelius’s book Meditations once introduced me to a fascinating figure from a long-past era. Beyond the fact that this man understood how to live life meaningfully, I was deeply surprised that he knew this so far back in history. Marcus lived nearly two thousand years ago, yet he understood the fundamental essence of human nature. I also find Epicurus’s writings useful and remarkable. After all, though centuries have passed and the world has transformed completely, has human nature truly changed?

A Meaningful Way of Life [#]

Why did Marcus Aurelius propose what many consider one of the most effective ways to live? Modern discoveries in psychotherapy, neuroscience, and personal experience have confirmed that negativity consumes a person’s inner joy and strips away the opportunity to live a peaceful, fulfilling life.

Sometimes, all the built-up negativity would leave my body after reading just a few lines of this book. Certain passages reawakened things I had long forgotten or dismissed as "naive and unacceptable in adulthood," or thoughts like, "They caused me too much pain to simply stop hating them." Yet I realize that it is far lighter to let go of those painful emotions and move forward.

Complementary Philosophies [#]

You can also draw valuable life principles from other schools of thought. Nihilism and Existentialism can offer peace of mind when you realize that there is no inherent grand meaning to existence and that we are merely tiny beings in a vast universe. This perspective brings comfort to some, though not to others; personally, I find a sense of freedom in the absence of absolute obligations to humanity or a higher entity. Combining the principles of other philosophies with Stoicism can be immensely helpful. Questioning norms is essential in any society and in every generation, as we must remember that social norms are established by people themselves.

A Psychological Support [#]

I have returned to Marcus Aurelius’s writings many times because they help me regain my balance, reevaluate my actions, and rethink how I view life and the people around me. I genuinely recommend that everyone read this book—or at least its first few pages—especially those who feel lost, abandoned, or lonely.

This book will not change your life at the snap of a finger, but it can serve as a powerful tool for self-reflection and self-help. If part of a therapist’s role is to guide a person toward healthier thinking patterns and help them see alternative perspectives where problems can be solved, this book fulfills that role remarkably well.
